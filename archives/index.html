Access requests

To view or copy archive records:

  1. Note the reference numbers of the records you need from the search above.
  2. Submit an access request.
  3. Pay the access fee.
  4. Receive your receipt by email.
  5. Receive approval to access the records.

Online access requests are coming soon. For now, please contact the Archives directly.
