Library rules

  • [EDIT] Members may borrow up to 3 items at a time.
  • [EDIT] Loan period is 2 weeks; items can be renewed once.
  • [EDIT] Reference and rare items may not leave the library.
  • [EDIT] Please keep noise low and no food near the collections.

Membership

Become a member to borrow books. The process:

  1. Fill in the membership form.
  2. Pay the membership fee.
  3. Receive your receipt by email.
  4. Your membership is approved and you can start borrowing.

Online membership applications are coming soon. For now, please apply in person at the library counter.
